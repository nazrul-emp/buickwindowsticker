VIN Structure

Understanding Your Buick's VIN

Every character in your 17-digit VIN tells a story. Here's what each segment reveals about your Buick.

Positions 1-3: World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI)

Country of origin, manufacturer, and vehicle type. Buick vehicles in the US start with 1G, 2G, 3G, 4G, or 5GA.

Positions 4-8: Vehicle Descriptor Section (VDS)

Model line, body style, engine type, restraint system, and transmission. This defines the core vehicle attributes.

Position 9: Check Digit

A mathematically calculated value that verifies the VIN is valid and has not been tampered with or altered.

Position 10: Model Year

Identifies the vehicle's model year. Letters and numbers rotate on a 30-year cycle (e.g., S=2025, T=2026, V=2027).

Position 11: Assembly Plant

Identifies the specific GM assembly plant where your Buick was manufactured (e.g., J = Lansing Delta Township, MI).

Positions 12-17: Vehicle Identifier Section (VIS)

The unique serial number of your vehicle. This is your Buick's individual production sequence within the model year.
