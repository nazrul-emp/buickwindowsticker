What Information Does a Window Sticker Contain?

Think of it as your Buick's day-one snapshot: pricing, equipment, packages, paint/interior codes, and more, straight from the build record.

Base price plus every paid package or standalone option, plus destination. Handy when negotiating or verifying premium claims on a used car.

Engine & Transmission Details

Displacement, cylinder count, induction type, horsepower, plus the transmission type and gearing. Where drivability differences begin.

The "comes with" list: safety features, infotainment, seating materials, lighting, wheels, and other standard equipment at that trim level.

Exterior & Interior Colors

Exact paint name and code (e.g., Moonstone Gray Metallic). Interior combination with trim codes for touch-up and bodywork.

City MPG, Highway MPG, Combined MPG, estimated annual fuel cost, and greenhouse-gas ratings for quick running cost comparisons.

Powertrain coverage, bumper-to-bumper coverage, roadside assistance, and corrosion coverage. Understand potential future costs.

VIN Location

Where Can You Find a VIN on a Buick Vehicle?

A VIN is a 17-character code that identifies the Buick. Some classic models may have shorter numbers.

You can spot it in a few easy places:

  • 01
    Dashboard (windshield corner)
    The most common location is a small plate visible through the windshield.
  • 02
    Driver-door jamb
    Open the driver's side door and look for a sticker on the doorpost where the door latches.
  • 03
    Engine block
    Many Buicks have the VIN stamped directly on the engine block.
  • 04
    Documents
    Your Buick's VIN appears on the registration, insurance card, service book, and delivery papers.
  • 05
    myBuick mobile app
    For newer, connected vehicles, the VIN is displayed within the app.
  • 06
    Classic models (pre-1981)
    May vary, so check multiple spots including frame stamps and body plates.
VIN Structure

How To Read a Buick VIN?

Every sticker is anchored by a unique 17-digit VIN. Here's a quick guide to the most important characters.

1-3

World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI)

Tells you this is a GM-built Buick and where it was made. The first three characters identify the manufacturer and country of origin.

4-8

Vehicle Descriptor Section (VDS)

Describes model, engine, body, and includes a check digit used to validate the VIN for authenticity.

10

Model Year

The 10th character represents the model year of the vehicle, encoded as a letter or number.

11

Plant Code

The 11th character identifies the assembly plant where the vehicle was manufactured.

12-17

Production Sequence

The final six digits are the unique production sequence number for this specific vehicle.
