FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Buick window stickers and VIN decoding.

A Buick Window Sticker (also known as a Monroney label) is the official document that lists the original MSRP, standard equipment, factory-installed options, packages, and specifications for your specific vehicle as it was built by General Motors.

Our data comes directly from General Motors' factory build database. Every window sticker is reconstructed from the original build record associated with your specific VIN, ensuring factory-level accuracy.

Your 17-character VIN can be found on your vehicle's dashboard (visible through the windshield on the driver's side), on the driver's side door jamb sticker, on your vehicle registration, or on your insurance card.

Delivery is instant. Once you enter your VIN and complete payment, your window sticker PDF is generated and available for immediate download. You will also receive a copy via email.

We support Buick models from 1980 to present. For older vehicles, data availability depends on GM's digital archives. Enter your VIN for a free compatibility check before purchasing.
